Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains how Rabbi Yisrael Frenkel handles your information on this website and in the tools he uses to run his practice. In short: your information is used only to serve you, it is kept private, and it is never sold or shared for advertising.

This website

You can read this website without giving any personal information. Some pages have forms, for example to register for a course or to share a testimonial. What you write there goes to Rabbi Frenkel, is used only for the purpose the form describes, and your words are quoted publicly only if you say so on the form.

Client Tracker (the practice's scheduling tool)

Client Tracker is a private tool that runs on Rabbi Frenkel's own computer. It is used only by him, for his own practice. It connects to his own Google account to:

  • Google Calendar: add, change and remove his own session appointments.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: keep one spreadsheet of his own sessions and payments, which the tool creates itself.
  • Gmail: send appointment confirmations, reminders and session summaries from his address to his clients.

The tool does not read anyone's email, and it does not open any other files or calendars. The connection to Google stays on his computer and is not shared with anyone.

Client Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

If you are a client

Your name, contact details, appointment times and session notes are kept privately for your care and for scheduling and payment. They are not shared with anyone without your permission, except where the law requires it. You may ask to see what is kept about you, or ask for it to be deleted.
